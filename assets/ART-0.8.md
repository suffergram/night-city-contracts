## Registration and runtime

`src/aim.js` defines per-frame shoulder socket positions, the arm-module pivot and scale,
and firearm barrel-tip anchors. The delivered arm drawings have different placement and scale
from the requested registration, so runtime explicitly registers them against the authored sockets.
The complete character is never rotated to aim: the locomotion layer stays upright, while only
arms/gun turn around the shoulder. Existing material recolouring applies to both layers.

Thirteen elevation angles cover −90° to +90° with a 15° step, mirrored for left facing.
The same geometry determines arm rotation, flash, bullet origin/direction and the aiming guide.
The bright guide point shows the actual firing direction after angle quantisation; the cursor
crosshair remains at the pointer. Weapon spread is applied after aiming.
The legacy complete clips remain active for reload, roll, block and katana actions.

Visual review: `../artifacts/night-city-aim-gallery.png` (all angles, standing, running,
backward movement, airborne and left-facing), generated by `scripts/browser-check.mjs`.
