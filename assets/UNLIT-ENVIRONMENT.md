## Rendering

Off-state art is the permanent base. Each cell is trimmed and registered to the original sprite bounds; gameplay dimensions, collision surfaces and per-style scale remain unchanged. Transparent silhouettes are clipped to the original alpha. Positive light contribution is extracted from the original illuminated texture against the off material and rendered additively in separate cached emitter layers. No opaque dark rectangles are drawn over a lit facade. Profile regions route light contributions to independent animation channels; they do not paint rectangular light sources. The remaining emission is steady.

Hotels, original props, market shops, location modules and everyday/industrial details use this pipeline. Existing distant backdrops keep their painted night lighting; they are not treated as interactive object sprites. Steam and dust remain independent of light switching.

`scripts/environment-preview.html` offers animated, all-off and all-on object lighting modes. No automated checks were run, following the user's preference.
