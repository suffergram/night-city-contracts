## Placement and rendering

The hub, industrial area and market now use a parked car; the port uses an armored van. Megablock retains its residential elevator on an upper corridor. The garage, service-door and freight-lift artwork above is retained for future dense building layouts in `BUILDING_EXIT_STYLES`, but is not selected for current outdoor contracts. Small conflicting decorative crates/fixtures are omitted; nearby street lamps and detail placement keep the access area clear. Vehicles keep their full scale even when parked in front of a narrow frontage.

`src/exits.js` defines native proportions, door/terminal regions, vehicle fixtures and source fixtures. `src/exit-render.js` preserves source aspect ratio. Vehicles use separate headlight beams, tail lamps, access-reader status and small exhaust plumes; no lights or smoke are baked into the art. The elevator animates its actual panel pixels inside a clipped opening after sector completion. Effects follow game time, pause and the environment preview's lighting controls. The previous energy-field sprite, moving horizontal energy lines and floating gate label are no longer rendered.

The E interaction, completion reward and choice between the next contract and hub are preserved. No automated checks were run per the user's preference.
